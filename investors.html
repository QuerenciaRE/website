High conviction and high alignment

The investors who work with us aren't looking for simple exposure to an asset class. They're looking for specific opportunities, presented conservatively, structured fairly, and managed by someone with skin in the game.

What Our Investors Seek

An operator who thinks like they do.

  • A high-touch operator who applies a bespoke, ground-up analysis to every opportunity
  • A sponsor aligned with the performance of the investment, not total assets under management
  • Investment analysis based on first principles and a willingness (but not necessity) to go against the status quo
  • Frequent re-assessment of where an asset sits in its life cycle and the capital markets cycle (every day an asset isn't sold, it is bought again)
  • A sponsor who protects the downside before reaching for the upside
  • Transparent, detailed investor communication from a sponsor who will not be mollified by good news and, whenever it does arrive, will share the bad news first
